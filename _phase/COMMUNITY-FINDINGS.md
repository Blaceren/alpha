## Final classification

**Credential findings (CM-14, CM-19) are PREPROD fixture hygiene, not Community product defects.**
In both cases product authentication behaved correctly (the app rejected a wrong password, exactly
as it should), roles and permissions were intact throughout, and only synthetic fixture credentials
had been overwritten — by this phase's own acceptance run. No production authentication behaviour
was weakened, no role was changed to make a test pass, and no permanent password-reset capability
was created. CM-15 is the related PREPROD tooling gap.

## Acceptance gate

**0 BLOCKER, 0 HIGH outstanding.** CM-01, CM-02, CM-03, CM-12 and CM-13 were all found by opening
the product in a real browser — three on desktop, two only at a genuine mobile layout viewport —
then fixed, rebuilt, republished, cut over and re-verified live.

Every one of the five was invisible to 1663 unit tests, a clean lint, a clean typecheck and a
passing production build. CM-13 in particular was invisible to a full desktop acceptance pass.
